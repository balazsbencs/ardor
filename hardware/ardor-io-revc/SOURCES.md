- [TI ADS1115](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads1115.pdf): address, PGA, pinout and input limits.
- [onsemi H11L1M family](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/h11l3m-d.pdf): optocoupler receiver.
- [Alpha & Omega AO3400A](https://www.aosmd.com/res/data_sheets/AO3400A.pdf): logic-level gate-drive guarantee and SOT-23 pinout.
- [Omron G5V-1](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g5v_1.pdf): 5 V coil, nominal 30 mA and 167 Ω.

- [Samsung CL21A226MAQNNN](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL21A226MAQNNN.do): 22 µF / 25 V, 0805 X5R.
- [Samsung CL21A106KAYNNN](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL21A106KAYNNN.do): 10 µF / 25 V, 0805 X5R.
- [Samsung CL31A106KBHNNN](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL31A106KBHNNN.do): 10 µF / 50 V, 1206 X5R.
