# T1 engineering review

Completed 8 October 2026 using KiCad 9.0.2. These are CAD and source reviews of unbuilt prototypes; no independent human approval or physical measurements are claimed.

| Review | Result and evidence |
|---|---|
| Circuit and substitutions | Compared the four reused circuits and connector assignments with M1. Checked semiconductor pinouts, supply ranges and relay contacts against the primary references in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md). The new LM386 headphone circuit has attenuation, local decoupling/bypass, output coupling, Zobel networks, bleeders and a grounded relay mute. |
| Breakout compatibility | Checked the exact Adafruit 1085 STEMMA QT Eagle layout against the custom footprint: both header rows, physical pin positions and logical functions. Carrier validation checks all 12 pad locations. Documented permanent onboard pull-ups, unused analog supply pins and address-jumper requirements. |
| Manual assembly | Checked component/BOM coverage, through-hole pad types and drills, package sizes, diode/capacitor polarity, IC orientation, component references and connector legends. All 138 electronic components have THT mounting; expression's purchased breakout contains preassembled SMD. Every project includes sockets/shunts/panel accessories separately. |
| Layout and drawings | Inspected all five schematic and assembly drawings plus copper and connector views. Corrected crowded headphone schematic labels, duplicate fabrication references and mounting-hole generation. MIDI has an explicit 3 mm loop-to-logic rule and ground-plane keepout. Native DRC includes courtyard, silkscreen, connectivity and schematic parity checks. |
| Native electrical checks | All five projects: zero ERC violations, zero DRC violations, zero unconnected items, zero schematic parity violations, no DRC exclusions. Independent wire-partition and netlist-to-pad checks cover all 349 numbered component pads. See [validation-summary.json](validation-summary.json). |
| Fabrication packages | Checked all 12 Gerber/drill files per module, outline dimensions, matching absolute drill origin, two NPTH mounting holes, ZIP CRC and exact ZIP/file contents. SMT placement exports have no rows. Per-project reports and SHA256 manifests identify the reviewed artifacts. |
| Regeneration | Rebuilt all five projects in a temporary copy using the documented scripts and frozen route sessions. Schematics, BOMs, guides and accessory lists match byte-for-byte. Board geometry, pad nets, tracks and legends match after ignoring generated UUIDs; filled copper has zero symmetric difference on both layers. The regenerated native and fabrication checks pass. See [regeneration-check.json](regeneration-check.json). |

Prototype commissioning remains necessary. Changed ferrites/TVS parts need cable and EMC/ESD measurements. MCP6022 bandwidth and the LM386 headphone architecture need audio/noise/stability measurements. The headphone host must keep enable LOW for at least five seconds after stable supply/audio, and mute before power-down. Measure startup and switching transients into dummy loads before connecting headphones. The boards are larger than M1 and require enclosure-clearance checks.
