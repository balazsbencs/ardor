# Compact T1.1 engineering review

Compact T1.1 and the three selected Hestore packages reviewed 12 October 2026 using KiCad 9.0.2. Original family reviewed 8 October; OPA1656 and low-voltage TVS updates reviewed 9 October. These are CAD and source reviews of unbuilt prototypes; no independent human approval or physical measurements are claimed.

| Review | Result and evidence |
|---|---|
| Circuit and substitutions | Compared the four reused circuits and connector assignments with M1. Checked semiconductor pinouts, supply ranges and relay contacts against the primary references in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md). The OPA1656 headphone circuit uses two inverting channels at −0.5 unloaded gain, a filtered 2.5 V reference, local decoupling, 100 pF feedback capacitors, 10 Ω output isolation, output coupling, bleeders and a grounded relay mute. Checked minimum-supply/1% divider common-mode margin and ideal load-dependent gain against actual native netlist values; these are calculations, not measured amplifier performance. |
| Breakout compatibility | Hestore ADS1115-M mechanical drawing establishes the 27.5 × 17 mm body and single ten-pin 2.54 mm header. New symbol/footprint omits Adafruit-only AVDD/AGND terminals and checks all ten physical pad positions against an independent header-order map. A0/A1 remain wiper/reference; A2/A3 remain grounded; ALRT stays unused. Onboard 10 kΩ I²C pull-ups and ADDR pull-down remain fitted; JP302 chooses GND or 3.3 V. Product/schematic ADS1115-versus-ADS1015 naming conflict is explicitly retained as a supplied-chip identity/commissioning check, including known inputs and conversion busy timing. |
| Hestore TVS packages | Six low-voltage positions retain DC Components 1.5KE6.8CA, HESTORE 100.430.71, 5.8 V standoff, 6.45–7.14 V breakdown and 10.5 V rated clamp. Custom axial footprints cover maximum 9.5 × 5.6 mm bodies and 1.07 mm leads at 15.24 mm pitch with 1.3 mm drills. MIDI D203/D204 now use the agreed Littelfuse SMDJ24CA, HESTORE 100.461.56, with 24 V standoff and 26.7–29.5 V breakdown. Custom SMC footprints cover the maximum body/terminal envelope and expose extended hand-solder pads. Both types are bidirectional with no cathode marking. Different capacitance/pulse characteristics remain cable/ESD test items; the board is not certified to the catalog peak-pulse rating. |
| Bipolar capacitor package | All nine coupling positions use the agreed Nichicon UES1H100MPM, 10 µF / 50 V, 3.5 mm pitch. Manufacturer drawing permits 8.5 mm maximum can diameter and 12.5 mm height. New unpolarized footprints use 0.8 mm drills for 0.6 mm leads and reserve the maximum body diameter. Nominal capacitances and calculated transfer functions remain unchanged. |
| Manual assembly | Checked component/BOM coverage, pad types/drills, package limits, polarity, orientation, component references and connector legends. Of 136 electronic components, 133 use THT mounting, two are agreed MIDI SMC TVS parts and one is the headphone OPA1656ID SOIC-8. Validation permits exactly those three loose SMD footprints. Expression's module contains preassembled SMD; builders fit one ten-pin header/socket. Upright feedback resistors and local bypass dimensions remain documented. Every project includes accessories separately. Front reference placement reserves actual Fab bodies and Silk/lead graphics, allowing readable text in exposed lead space; narrow references can rotate without shrinking the 0.9 mm lettering. |
| Compact placement | Replaced 2.5× scaled SMD coordinates with explicit functional THT placements. MIDI 85×70 → 58×42 mm; expression 110×85 → 72×50; mixer 100×75 → 70×48; line 115×85 → 70×50; headphones 96×84 → 76×58. Area reduction is 45–64%. All actual embedded courtyards, including maximum capacitor bodies, ADC module and mounting holes, remain disjoint and within the outline with 0.25 mm margin. See [compact-layouts.json](compact-layouts.json). |
| Layout and drawings | Inspected all five schematic and assembly drawings plus copper and connector views. Inspected headphone component/connector legends, SOIC pin-1 orientation, capacitor polarity and relay labels. Locked local front-layer feedback and supply-bypass routes are checked against the final PCB copper, allowing only numerical rounding/splitting. MIDI has an explicit 3 mm loop-to-logic rule and ground-plane keepout. Native DRC includes courtyard, silkscreen, connectivity and schematic parity checks. |
| Native electrical checks | All five projects: zero ERC violations, zero DRC violations, zero unconnected items, zero schematic parity violations, no DRC exclusions. Independent wire-partition and netlist-to-pad checks cover all 337 numbered component pads (325 THT, twelve SMD). The two removed pads are the former Adafruit-only terminals; all retained pin functions and host/panel interfaces are preserved. See [validation-summary.json](validation-summary.json) and [headphone circuit calculations/local copper](../headphones/verification/headphone-circuit.json). |
| Fabrication packages | Regenerated all 12 Gerber/drill files per module, compact outlines, absolute drill origin and two NPTH M2 holes. ZIP CRC and exact archive/file content checks pass. Three placement exports are empty; MIDI contains exactly D203/D204 and headphones exactly U601. These exports support manual assembly, not an SMT production order. Per-project reports and manifests identify the reviewed artifacts. |
| Regeneration | Rebuilt all five projects in a temporary copy using the documented scripts and frozen route sessions. Schematics, BOMs, guides and accessories match byte-for-byte. Board geometry, pad nets, tracks and legends match exactly after ignoring generated UUIDs. Filled copper matches within two native nanometre coordinate units: MIDI bottom copper has 0.0000001365 mm² symmetric difference and about 1.35 nm maximum boundary drift; all other layer area differences are zero. The comparison bounds both displacement and total changed area, while retaining exact non-fill geometry checks. Regenerated native and fabrication checks pass. See [regeneration-check.json](regeneration-check.json). |

Prototype commissioning remains necessary. Changed ferrites/TVS parts need cable and EMC/ESD measurements. MCP6022 bandwidth and OPA1656 headphone drive need audio/noise/stability measurements, including actual headphone cables, representative capacitance, both supply limits and insertion shorts. The headphone host must keep enable LOW for at least five seconds after stable supply/audio, and mute before power-down. Measure startup and switching transients into dummy loads before connecting headphones. The boards are larger than M1 and require enclosure-clearance checks.
