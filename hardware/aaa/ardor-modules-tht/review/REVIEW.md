# T1 engineering review

Original family reviewed 8 October 2026; OPA1656 and Hestore TVS updates reviewed 9 October 2026 using KiCad 9.0.2. These are CAD and source reviews of unbuilt prototypes; no independent human approval or physical measurements are claimed.

| Review | Result and evidence |
|---|---|
| Circuit and substitutions | Compared the four reused circuits and connector assignments with M1. Checked semiconductor pinouts, supply ranges and relay contacts against the primary references in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md). The OPA1656 headphone circuit uses two inverting channels at −0.5 unloaded gain, a filtered 2.5 V reference, local decoupling, 100 pF feedback capacitors, 10 Ω output isolation, output coupling, bleeders and a grounded relay mute. Checked minimum-supply/1% divider common-mode margin and ideal load-dependent gain against actual native netlist values; these are calculations, not measured amplifier performance. |
| Breakout compatibility | Checked the exact Adafruit 1085 STEMMA QT Eagle layout against the custom footprint: both header rows, physical pin positions and logical functions. Carrier validation checks all 12 pad locations. Documented permanent onboard pull-ups, unused analog supply pins and address-jumper requirements. |
| Hestore TVS replacement | All six SA5.0CA positions use DC Components 1.5KE6.8CA, HESTORE 100.430.71. Checked 5.8 V stand-off, 6.45–7.14 V breakdown and 10.5 V rated clamp against the manufacturer, rather than the distributor's ambiguous 6.8 V description. Custom bidirectional footprint covers the 9.5 × 5.6 mm maximum body and 1.07 mm leads at 15.24 mm pitch with 1.3 mm drills. Removed cathode markings; either orientation is valid. Enlarged packages require fresh routing; headphone placements were moved for edge and component clearance. MIDI D203/D204 retain SA24CA because replacing their higher-voltage protection with 1.5KE6.8CA would change normal operation. Different pulse currents and capacitance prevent a claim of equivalent ESD/audio behavior without measurements. |
| Manual assembly | Checked component/BOM coverage, through-hole pad types and drills, package sizes, diode/capacitor polarity, IC orientation, component references and connector legends. Of 136 electronic components, 135 have THT mounting and one is the agreed headphone OPA1656ID SOIC-8 exception. It has eight 1.27 mm-pitch exposed leads and no thermal pad; the validation permits no other loose SMD parts. Expression's purchased breakout contains preassembled SMD. Upright feedback resistors and the local bypass dimensions are documented. Every project includes sockets/shunts/panel accessories separately. |
| Layout and drawings | Inspected all five schematic and assembly drawings plus copper and connector views. Inspected headphone component/connector legends, SOIC pin-1 orientation, capacitor polarity and relay labels. Locked local front-layer feedback and supply-bypass routes are checked against the final PCB copper, allowing only numerical rounding/splitting. MIDI has an explicit 3 mm loop-to-logic rule and ground-plane keepout. Native DRC includes courtyard, silkscreen, connectivity and schematic parity checks. |
| Native electrical checks | All five projects: zero ERC violations, zero DRC violations, zero unconnected items, zero schematic parity violations, no DRC exclusions. Independent wire-partition and netlist-to-pad checks cover all 339 numbered component pads (331 THT, eight SOIC). See [validation-summary.json](validation-summary.json) and [headphone circuit calculations/local copper](../headphones/verification/headphone-circuit.json). |
| Fabrication packages | Checked all 12 Gerber/drill files per module, outline dimensions, matching absolute drill origin, two NPTH mounting holes, ZIP CRC and exact ZIP/file contents. Four SMT placement exports have no rows; the headphone export contains exactly U601. All boards are supplied as bare-board fabrication packages for manual assembly. Per-project reports and SHA256 manifests identify the reviewed artifacts. |
| Regeneration | Rebuilt all five projects in a temporary copy using the documented scripts and frozen route sessions. Schematics, BOMs, guides and accessory lists match byte-for-byte. Board geometry, pad nets, tracks and legends match after ignoring generated UUIDs; filled copper has zero symmetric difference on both layers. The regenerated native and fabrication checks pass. See [regeneration-check.json](regeneration-check.json). |

Prototype commissioning remains necessary. Changed ferrites/TVS parts need cable and EMC/ESD measurements. MCP6022 bandwidth and OPA1656 headphone drive need audio/noise/stability measurements, including actual headphone cables, representative capacitance, both supply limits and insertion shorts. The headphone host must keep enable LOW for at least five seconds after stable supply/audio, and mute before power-down. Measure startup and switching transients into dummy loads before connecting headphones. The boards are larger than M1 and require enclosure-clearance checks.
