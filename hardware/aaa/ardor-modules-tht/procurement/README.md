# Hestore purchasing audit

Checked **9 October 2026** against all five T1 hand-assembly `BOM.csv` files and their `assembly/accessories.csv` files at commit `0c4af022026dd765e34e61d4f4fbc2dfaa6a2553`. This covers **136 component positions and 22 accessory entries**, for one of each board. The separate M1 SMD production BOMs are outside this audit.

Most components have a stocked Hestore listing, but **the current PCBs cannot be sourced entirely from Hestore with verified substitutions**. This audit proposes purchasing alternatives; it does not modify schematics, footprints, board values, Gerbers or the authoritative BOMs. A candidate marked for review must not be treated as an approved replacement.

Stock figures are the site's displayed lower bounds, not reserved quantities. `Raktáron` means on hand; `3 munkanap` and `10–15 munkanap` mean supplier lead times in workdays. A blank on-hand listing is recorded as `not listed`, not inferred to be stocked. Recheck before buying. “Not found” means the searched catalog did not yield the specified item; it does not establish that the manufacturer discontinued it. Generic listings without a manufacturer/body drawing remain conditional even when the value and pitch match.

## Available main devices and ordinary passives

The exact [OPA1656ID](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10043729.html), [MCP6022-I/P](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10037299.html), [G5V-2-H1 5VDC](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10034426.html), [G5V-1 5VDC](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10041522.html) and agreed [DC Components 1.5KE6.8CA](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10043071.html) are listed on hand. Keep the high-sensitivity **H1** headphone relay variant: the ordinary G5V-2 has a different coil current.

Hestore's [Semtech Electronics 2N3904](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10041447.html) is an alternative to onsemi 2N3904BU. Its [manufacturer drawing](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=14196) confirms emitter/base/collector at pins 1/2/3; form the leads to the board's wide TO-92 pitch. Hestore's [Everlight H11L1](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10043531.html) is an alternative to onsemi H11L1M. The [Everlight datasheet](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=14421) confirms the same functional pinout, standard DIP-6, a 3–15 V operating supply, maximum 1.6 mA turn-on threshold and maximum 4 µs switching times at its documented 5 V conditions. This fits the existing 5 V MIDI receiver topology and 31.25 kbaud operation on paper; incoming package identity and bench operation still need checking. Everlight's **M** suffix means wider leads, unlike onsemi's naming: order the standard Everlight DIP variant.

[onsemi/Fairchild 1N4148](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10023064.html) is the same diode type in DO-35, replacing the BOM's Vishay brand. Hestore's [BAT85 listing](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10022238.html) attaches a [Vishay BAT85S datasheet](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=785), but does not spell out BAT85S in its title. Confirm the supplied variant before accepting it for the four expression clamps.

All eleven nonzero resistor values are listed as **1% metal film, 0.4 W**, meeting the required 0.25 W rating. Six listings publish a 3.7 × 1.9 mm body and 0.45 mm leads, fitting within the current footprint envelopes. The other five values lack published dimensions; the CSV marks those for confirmation. Bend horizontal leads to 10.16 mm. Use the small [10 kΩ 0.4 W part](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10025387.html) for upright headphone R651/R652 at 2.54 mm pitch. The four R101 positions can be formed wire links.

The selected polarized electrolytics retain capacitance, meet or exceed voltage ratings, and match published diameter/pitch requirements. The CSV includes their body heights for enclosure checking. Standard breakable headers, 2×3 headers, female six-pin strips, shunts and DIP sockets are available.

The supplier's [ADS1115-M dimension drawing](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=16090) shows a **27.5 × 17 mm board, one ten-pin 2.54 mm row** ordered VDD, GND, SCL, SDA, ADDR, ALRT, A0, A1, A2, A3. This differs from the present two six-pin rows. Its [schematic](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=13058) shows 10 kΩ pull-ups on SCL/SDA/ALRT and a 10 kΩ ADDR pull-down, but the ADC symbol reads ADS1015 while its MPN caption reads ADS1115IDGST. A new carrier must map functions, account for address/pull resistors, and use a confirmed 16-bit ADS1115 module. The MINI listing has no verified module-mechanical drawing in this audit. Retaining the existing Adafruit board avoids these unresolved changes.

For bipolar capacitors, [Nichicon's UES table](https://www.nichicon.co.jp/english/products/pdfs/e-ues.pdf) confirms the larger 10 µF / 50 V body. The [Panasonic SU datasheet](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=17529) confirms 22 µF / 25 V bipolar construction, 5 × 11 mm body and 2.5 mm pitch. It marks Japan as obsolete, so its displayed inventory should not be assumed to establish long-term replenishment. Nine are needed for the family, and the displayed on-hand quantity covers one set. At unchanged resistance, 22 µF lowers an ideal coupling pole by `10/22` and lengthens its time constant by `22/10`. For the headphone 20 kΩ input, the pole changes from about 0.796 Hz to 0.362 Hz and the time constant from 0.20 s to 0.44 s. This does not independently establish startup silence; re-evaluate the existing five-second host mute delay and test switching transients. Ordinary polarized electrolytics are not substitutes for these bipolar positions.

The [CT4 manufacturer drawing](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=24624) defines the leaded C0G part's 4.2 mm maximum length, 3.8 mm maximum height/thickness and 5.08 mm pitch. The [CCH drawing](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=15898) confirms the 1 kV candidate's 7 mm diameter, 2.5 mm thickness and SL dielectric. Both need package review before being added to an authoritative BOM. The [Littelfuse SMDJ datasheet](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=16974) establishes SMDJ24CA's voltage class and maximum overall 8.13 × 6.22 mm SMC envelope. It is a higher-power TVS with different capacitance; matching the voltage table does not prove equivalent MIDI cable/transient behavior.

The [Diotec 1.5KE datasheet supplied by Hestore](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_getfile.php?id=871) confirms the alternative 1.5KE33CA's 28.2 V standoff and 45.7 V rated clamp. The listing allows Diotec or Littelfuse supply, so any future adoption must select and check the actual manufacturer's body/lead drawing.

## Assembly accessories

[DIP6](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10023131.html), [DIP8](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10023132.html), [male strips](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10026161.html), [female six-pin strips](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10029494.html), [2×3 headers](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10034608.html) and [shunts](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10041805.html) are stocked. Component CSV header quantities count **cut segments**, not whole 40-pin strips. Purchase enough complete strips for those segments plus breakout accessories; exact female six-pin strips avoid cutting through socket housings.

Stocked wired panel-jack options are [6.3 mm TRS for expression](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10022369.html), [3.5 mm TRS for headphones](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10022350.html), and [6.3 mm TS for line out](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10022362.html). Match the enclosure, identify tip/ring/sleeve contacts and leave unused switch contacts open. The [DIN5P180 panel socket](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10043421.html) is available, but insulated-shell mounting is not established by the listing. Confirm shell/panel insulation and follow the MIDI chassis-bond guide rather than joining it to signal ground.

Exact 32 Ω / ≥0.5 W dummy loads were not found. Stocked [33 Ω / 1% / 0.6 W resistors](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10020482.html) make practical external preliminary test loads; calculate using 33 Ω rather than calling them exact 32 Ω loads. This does not replace cable, insertion-short or actual 32 Ω headphone testing. These resistors are test accessories, not components to fit to the PCB.

**M2 nonconductive standoffs remain an accessory gap.** Hestore's [M2 brass](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10043196.html) and [M2 metal](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10040560.html) parts do not meet that requirement. Its [nylon kit](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10051822.html) contains M2.5/M3 sizes, incompatible with the boards' 2.2 mm holes. Source M2 nylon hardware elsewhere and choose height/screws for the enclosure. [Insulated hookup wire](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10036639.html) and [female host connector strips](https://www.hestore.hu/prod_10028058.html) are stocked; quantities and insulation/mounting depend on the host and enclosure.

## How to use this audit

Order `exact part`, `compatible` and `compatible brand replacement` candidates only after reading their assembly notes and checking the enclosure. Resolve `confirm…` entries with the supplier's actual part/drawing. Resolve `PCB…` entries through design review and regenerated manufacturing files before substitution. `no verified…` entries require another supplier or a reviewed redesign. The grouped CSV retains separate BOM specifications even when they share a candidate SKU, so sum repeated SKUs when preparing an order and distinguish on-board parts from accessories. Alternative links are choices, not additional required quantities.

The least disruptive purchasing route is Hestore for the available parts, with the exact Adafruit carrier, axial ferrites, small bipolar capacitors, MIDI SA24CA/1 kV C0G parts and M2 nylon hardware from another supplier, plus confirmation of the generic ceramics. For broader Hestore sourcing, first review the larger 10 µF bipolar footprint and the wider leaded C0G feedback capacitor; then decide whether to redesign the ADC carrier and add large SMC MIDI TVS parts. No all-Hestore / all-THT replacement is established for the ferrites by this audit.
