three directions

Complete visual worlds for the 1280×720 device screen, each carried across four screens — preset, edit chain, tuner, parameters. Every function of the current build is preserved; the layout, hierarchy and metaphor are not.

Panel (D) is the working direction. Start with the palette study — three colour systems on identical geometry. Cyc and Sleeve are its two live alternates. Floor, Plate and Sheet are the first round, kept for reference; Plate was ruled out and the other two superseded, so their known layout defects are unfixed.

The banding is a hardware fact, not a tuning problem

lv_conf.h:13 sets LV_COLOR_DEPTH 16 on the device because the Pi DSI framebuffer is RGB565; the simulator runs at 32. RGB565 gives 32 levels of red, 64 of green, 32 of blue, with no dithering in LVGL's render path. The current palette ramps #07090b → #18232a — a dark, low-contrast, full-panel gradient, which is the worst case for a 5-bit channel. It cannot be made smooth.

All three directions therefore build depth from flat planes at different values, hairlines and hard edges. None of them contains a gradient.

E · Cyc

A preset is a lighting cue and the screen is the cyclorama behind the player. Only the running cue is lit; the others sit in blackout with one hairline. A cyc broken into its individual fixtures — discrete bands, no blend.

F · Sleeve

The quietest one. Hierarchy carried entirely by scale and empty space — the running preset is simply set four times larger. No cards, no buttons, no filled panels anywhere. Lowercase throughout, and the chain runs vertically instead of horizontally.

4 screens · record-sleeve restraint · near-black + one blue

A · Floor

The setlist taped to the stage floor. Matte gaffer black, white paint-pen lettering at poster scale, saturated console tape carrying every family and state. The live preset is a bright tape strip with black lettering — the highest-contrast object on a dark stage.

4 screens · assigned direction · warm black + paint + tape colour

B · Plate

One bit. Black and white pixels only, Bayer-ordered stipple standing in for every gray, effect families carried by pattern instead of hue. Dithering is the canonical answer to a 16-bit panel, taken as a whole design language. The live preset does not tint — it inverts.

4 screens · dealt challenger · pure monochrome

C · Sheet

The pedal shows you the schematic. Reference designators, net labels, a title block, revision triangles, tolerance bands. The preset screen draws four miniature signal-flow diagrams, so you see the shape of each rig before choosing it — and bypass is drawn as a jumper routed around the block.

4 screens · engineering drafting sheet · petrol ink + signal yellow
