1. Lamp Black

The lowest-risk upgrade. Keep the Panel language, but widen the value range from near-black to a fully flooded lamp tile, and let each preset show its chain in colour.

What changes

  • Ground drops from #212528 to #0b0c0d. Plates sit clearly above it.
  • The live preset floods the whole tile with the lamp. You can see it from 1.5 m without reading.
  • Every tile carries a family-colour chain strip, so the four slots stop looking identical.
  • The header shows the bank, not latency. Latency moves to a small readout on the right.
  • Master is a numeral plus a segment meter in bone. Red stays reserved for LIVE.

LVGL build notes

  • Token change in LvglUiStyle.cpp plus one new tile layout. Nothing needs new widgets.
  • Chain strip: a flex row of flat lv_obj segments with flex_grow weights.
  • Selected block lift: a solid dark lv_obj offset 8 px behind the card. No blur, so no banding on RGB565.
  • Bypass hatch: a small pre-built RGB565 tile image, as the spec already asks (law 7).
  • Module drawer keeps kBlockDrawerWidth (480 px). The dim layer is one flat lv_obj at fixed opacity, with no fade.
  • Parameter travel scale: an lv_bar for the fill, an lv_scale for the ticks and a flat handle object. lv_slider with custom parts can also do it.
