2. Enclosures

The most colourful direction. Each preset is a painted stompbox. Players pick the colour when they save, so a set list becomes a row of recognisable pedals.

What changes

  • Preset colour becomes a saved preset field (8 swatches). This is a schema addition, so it needs your approval.
  • Inactive presets show a dark shade of their colour. The live preset shows the full colour, a bone frame and a lit jewel.
  • Hard offset plates under each enclosure give real depth with zero blur.
  • In the editor, blocks become small pedals with knob pointers and a status LED, joined by patch cables.

LVGL build notes

  • Enclosure: lv_obj with radius 14 and a solid offset lv_obj behind it. Circles are LV_RADIUS_CIRCLE.
  • Footswitch: three stacked flat circles. The knob pointer is an lv_line with a set angle.
  • Floor texture: one 6 x 6 px RGB565 tile image, repeated with lv_image tiling.
  • Risk: 8 preset colours must each pass 4.5:1 with dark ink. Check each swatch on the device panel.
