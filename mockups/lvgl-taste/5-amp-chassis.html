5. Amp Chassis

The warmest direction. Presets are engraved faceplates on tolex, the live one is polished chrome with a lit pilot jewel, and the tuner is a real VU meter.

What changes

  • Surfaces get material: tolex ground, brushed plates, chrome for the live preset. This is the biggest break from the flat Panel language.
  • Engraving is two 1 px hard offsets, one light and one dark. It reads as depth but has no blur.
  • A pilot jewel on each plate is the LIVE lamp. Only the live one glows red.
  • Master becomes a chicken-head style knob with a printed scale. The tuner becomes a VU face with a needle.

LVGL build notes

  • Textures: tolex and brushed-metal tiles, dithered offline to RGB565 and tiled with lv_image. The dither is baked in, so no banding.
  • Engraved text: two extra lv_label copies offset by 1 px. That triples label count, so use it only for preset names.
  • VU needle: one lv_line rotated with lv_obj_set_style_transform_rotation. The face is a static image.
  • Risk: this look depends on image assets. Each palette would need its own texture set.
