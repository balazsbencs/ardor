Quality and routing changes covered by this run
- Plate processes every host sample at 48 kHz and reports zero resampler latency.
- Plate delay/tap lengths and modulation depth retain their physical timing at 48 kHz.
- The bright Plate response retains useful 14 kHz energy instead of crossing the
  old 24 kHz adapter's low-pass boundary.
- Room and Hall use 8 delay lines with signed orthogonal input/output projections.
- Asymmetric early-reflection timing prevents mono sources from collapsing into
  identical left/right fields.
- Regression gates require Room correlation < 0.70 and density > 0.95, Hall
  correlation < 0.70 and density > 0.90, and bounded responses at all endpoints.
