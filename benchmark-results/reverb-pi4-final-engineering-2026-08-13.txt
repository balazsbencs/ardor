Implemented after the native-Plate / 8-line-FDN pass
- All physical reverb controls are smoothed over 20 ms; FDN geometry taps also
  ramp across control blocks. Automation regression tests reject large tap steps.
- Room Size scales early-reflection and all eight late-tank delay lengths from
  65% to 135% while recomputing feedback gain for stable displayed decay.
- Room, Hall, and Plate default measured broadband RT60 now track 2.0 s.
- Spring adds a dwell-dependent launch transient, frequency-dependent pickup
  loss, and a decorrelated pickup matrix. Correlation fell from 0.913 to 0.269.
- Magneto uses independent right-head timing, cross-feedback, and cross-diffusion.
  Correlation fell from 1.000 to 0.033 and 200-500 ms density rose 0.652 to 0.815.
- Bloom, Chorale, Nonlinear, and Swell trims were calibrated using burst response
  while retaining their intended slow/gated envelopes and at least 9.5 dB peak headroom.
